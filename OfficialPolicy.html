Privacy Policy

CueFloat: Video Teleprompter • Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to CueFloat: Video Teleprompter ("CueFloat", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy describes how CueFloat handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play.

Offline-First Design: CueFloat is an offline-first teleprompter utility. All your scripts, notes, and custom configurations remain strictly on your local device. We do not operate external script storage servers or sell your personal information.

1. Summary of Key Privacy Principles

  • No Account Required: You can create, edit, and prompt scripts without registering an account.
  • Scripts Stay on Your Device: All scripts are stored in your device's local database.
  • No Camera Usage in this Version: CueFloat does not access, record, or capture video from your camera.
  • Real-Time On-Device Voice Processing: When voice-activated scroll is active, speech is processed locally in real-time and never recorded or transmitted.

2. Permissions Requested & Why They Are Needed

CueFloat requests only the specific Android permissions necessary to deliver its core teleprompter capabilities:

A. Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Purpose: Allows the teleprompter window to float over your device's camera, video-meeting, or social streaming apps while you record.

Data Safety: This permission is used exclusively to draw the floating text window. CueFloat never monitors, captures, inspects, or reads your screen or any data from other apps running underneath.

B. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS - Android 13+)

Purpose: Displays an ongoing status bar notification while the prompter is running.

Control & Dismissal: This permission is used exclusively to provide you with ongoing playback controls (Play, Pause) and an instant Exit button to completely dismiss/close the floating overlay at any time. We do not send marketing, promotional, or advertising notifications.

C. Foreground Services (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, specialUse & microphone)

Purpose: Keeps the floating teleprompter responsive and active while you navigate to your camera app, preventing Android from abruptly killing the prompter window in the background.

Data Safety: Used strictly for overlay window lifecycle and optional on-device voice processing. No personal data is transmitted.

D. Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO - Optional)

Purpose: Used only when you voluntarily enable voice-assisted features (such as on-device Voice-Activated Auto-Scroll or the live audio level meter).

Data Safety: All audio is processed strictly on-device in real-time. Audio is never recorded to permanent files, stored, uploaded, or transmitted to any server.

E. Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT)

Purpose: Allows you to connect wireless Bluetooth presentation remotes, selfie clickers, or headset buttons to control prompter scrolling hands-free (Play/Pause, speed adjustment).

F. Vibration (VIBRATE)

Purpose: Delivers subtle haptic feedback during countdown ticks and remote control clicks.

G. Storage & Document Access

Purpose: When you choose to import an existing script (.txt, .md), CueFloat uses Android's native system file picker to read only the specific document you select.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

CueFloat may integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information, such as your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and general diagnostic logs to serve relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services by visiting: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All scripts and preferences reside solely on your device. When you delete a script inside CueFloat, it is removed immediately. If you clear app data or uninstall CueFloat, all locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

CueFloat is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated revision date.
