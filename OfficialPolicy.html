Privacy Policy

CueFloat: Video Teleprompter • Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to CueFloat: Video Teleprompter ("CueFloat", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data privacy when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

CueFloat is built as an offline-first, privacy-focused creator utility. Your scripts, presentations, and personal content belong solely to you.

1. Summary of Key Principles

  • No Account Required: You can use CueFloat completely without signing up or creating an account.
  • Scripts Stay on Your Device: All your scripts, drafts, and preferences are stored strictly locally in on-device storage.
  • No Cloud Uploads: We never upload your text to external servers, nor do we sell or monetize your data.
  • No Camera Usage in this Version: The current version of CueFloat does not access, capture, or record through your device's camera.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal Identity Information: We do not collect names, phone numbers, or emails.
  • User Content: We cannot read, access, or transmit your scripts or notes.
  • Location Data: We do not track or request your GPS or network location.
  • Advertising / Tracking Identifiers: We do not bundle third-party ad networks or tracking trackers.

3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

CueFloat requests only the minimum Android permissions required for its teleprompter floating functions:

A. Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Allows CueFloat to display the movable, resizable floating reading overlay over your phone's camera app or streaming app while you speak. CueFloat does not record or read the screen of other apps.

B. Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE & specialUse)

Required by Android to keep the floating prompter smooth and active while you switch apps or record in camera apps, preventing the operating system from terminating the overlay unexpectedly.

C. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Displays an ongoing playback notification (Play, Pause, Close) in your notification shade so you can control prompting easily.

D. Audio / Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO - Optional)

If you choose to enable voice-assisted features (such as voice-activated auto-scroll or live audio level meters), audio is processed strictly on-device in real-time. Audio is never recorded to disk, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.

E. Storage / File Access

If you choose to import a .txt or .md script, the app uses Android's native system file picker to read only the specific document you selected.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, deleting a script from the app deletes it from your device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local app data.

5. Third-Party Services

CueFloat operates offline. When distributed through Google Play, standard Google Play Services may provide anonymized crash and performance diagnostics (via Google Play Console) to help resolve app issues.
